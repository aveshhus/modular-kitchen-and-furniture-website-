We design and manufacture all configurations including open-concept Island suites, high-efficiency Parallel kitchens, luxury U-Shaped layouts, and space-optimized L-Shaped kitchens. We engineer the classic golden work triangle (Hob, Sink, Refrigerator) with millimeter-accurate clearances, integrated German Gola handleless profiles, pantry pull-outs, corner carousels, and dedicated appliance garages with motorized or rolling shutters.
✦ Island & Parallel Suites ✦ German Gola Handleless ✦ Golden Triangle Ergonomics ✦ Appliance Garages
Modular kitchens and wardrobes in Indian households face high moisture, water spillage, and heavy countertop loads (such as granite or quartz). 100% Calibrated IS 710 Boiling Water Proof (BWP) Marine Plywood uses hardwood veneers bonded with fortified phenolic resins under extreme pressure. It is 100% borer and termite proof, never sags under heavy utensil weight, and will not swell or delaminate over decades.
✦ 100% Calibrated IS 710 ✦ Zero Swelling / Delamination ✦ Borer & Termite Immune ✦ Zero Particle Board
Hinged wardrobes provide 100% unobstructed panoramic access with 180° wide-opening hinges. Top-hung sliding wardrobes save precious floor space in compact bedrooms without bottom floor channel dust accumulation. Bronze or fluted aluminum profile glass shutters deliver luxury showroom elegance with vertical concealed LED illumination, velvet jewelry organizers, and pull-down hydraulic wardrobe lifters.
✦ Bronze Glass Profile Doors ✦ Top-Hung Floor-Free Sliding ✦ Hydraulic Wardrobe Lifters ✦ Velvet Accessory Trays
We fabricate architectural entertainment walls with concealed zero-wire chase conduits behind seamless Italian porcelain slabs, fluted teak acoustic slats, or micro-cement paneling. Floating media credenzas are mounted on heavy-duty cantilever steel anchors (tested for 150kg+ loads), featuring acoustic fabric front grilles to conceal gaming consoles, AV receivers, and subwoofers with seamless remote pass-through.
✦ Zero-Wire Conduits ✦ Italian Porcelain Backdrops ✦ Teak Acoustic Slats ✦ 150kg Cantilever Mounts
We partner exclusively with Blum (Austria) and Häfele (Germany). Every drawer uses Blum Tandembox or Legrabox soft-close runners rated for 200,000 motion cycles, and overhead units feature Aventos HK-S lift-ups. All mechanisms come with our comprehensive 10-Year In-House Warranty backed by our local Jaipur technician support team.
✦ Blum Austria Certified ✦ Häfele Germany ✦ 200,000 Motion Cycles ✦ 10-Year In-House Warranty
Bathroom cabinetry is fabricated exclusively from 100% IS 710 marine substrate sealed with high-grade PUR (Polyurethane Reactive) hot-melt edge-banding, creating a completely waterproof joint. We integrate engineered quartz tops, fluted ceramic basins, stainless-steel Blum runners, and anti-fog LED backlit smart touch mirrors.
✦ PUR Waterproof Hot-Melt ✦ Quartz Basin Tops ✦ Smart Anti-Fog Mirrors ✦ Stainless Steel Hardware
